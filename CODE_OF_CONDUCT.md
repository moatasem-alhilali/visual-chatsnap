# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). The summary below is a convenience; the linked text is authoritative.

## Our pledge

We, as members, contributors and maintainers, pledge to make participation in this community a harassment-free experience for everyone. That holds regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive and healthy community, in Arabic, English or any other language.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being kind and respectful, including toward people who disagree with you
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Taking responsibility for our mistakes and learning from them
- Focusing on what is best for the community and its users

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention or advances
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information, such as real chat screenshots, phone numbers or addresses, without their explicit permission
- Using this project or its spaces to create or share deceptive content aimed at real people
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all community spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews) and when an individual officially represents the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer privately through the contact options on the [maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/moatasem-alhilali), or by opening a [private security advisory](https://github.com/moatasem-alhilali/visual-chatsnap/security/advisories/new) if the report is sensitive. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, and the reporter's privacy will be respected.

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that don't align with this Code of Conduct. They may also temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behaviour they deem harmful, following the [Contributor Covenant enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines): correction, warning, temporary ban, permanent ban.
